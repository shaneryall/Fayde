    References¶

    Every algorithm Fayde uses, where it comes from, and where it lives in the code. When an algorithm has no published source, the entry says so. Keep this file in step with the code: a change that adds or replaces an algorithm updates its entry here, and the implementing module's doc comment cites the same source.

    Wider survey and verdicts: docs/research/literature-review.md.

    Fayde's own choices (no published source)¶

    Choice Rationale Code
    Incremental Cartesian unit-vector buckets of side at least the separation chord, searching 27 neighbouring buckets and checking exact distances Bounded local searches for streaming insertion/replacement and pattern thinning, without rebuilding the static k-d tree; independent of HEALPix boundaries crates/fayde-index/src/build.rs (SeparationGrid)
    High-proper-motion cut: drop a star from patterns if |μ| × 25 yr > 0.5 × pattern_max_error × fov/2 A shift δ changes ratios by ≈ δ / (largest edge); keeps motion-induced ratio error within half a bin over the index lifetime crates/fayde-index/src/build.rs
    Default proper-motion error 50 mas/yr where UCAC4 has none Value UCAC4 itself uses for "no data" in output tables (readme note 9) crates/fayde-catalog/src/ucac4.rs
    Strict-by-default catalogue queries (missing zone = error) An empty cone must never be mistaken for empty sky crates/fayde-catalog/src/lib.rs
    Pre-filter: ≥ 2 of the next 12 ranked sources must land on seed-list catalogue stars within 3σ + 2 px + 1% of distance from the seed pair Tolerance grows with distance because a 2-star seed's rotation/scale error does crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (prefilter)
    Reference count per hypothesis: brightest clamp(round(1.5 × Σ p_star), 10, 2000) catalogue stars in the frame, using usable sources after duplicate demotion (p_star defaults to 1) Effective real-source count prevents low-probability detections from inflating background density; placeholder until detection_limit is supported (design doc §6.2) crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs
    Local-refit radii 12 → 6 → 3 px; similarity fit below 8 matches, affine from 8 Tuned on synthetic fields; four to seven stars cannot constrain shear and anisotropy crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (local_refit, rematch_fit)
    A polynomial order above 1 is a BIC candidate only with four observations per parameter, n ≥ 4k (24 matches for order 2, 40 for order 3); n ≥ 2k while a tune-up is still collecting matches, whose result is refitted under the strict rule The profiled BIC has no floor: as k approaches n the fit interpolates, χ² → 0 and n·ln(χ²/n) → −∞. Measured without a rule, hinted solves of sparse frames: 43 of 60 frames with 6 stars and 52 of 60 with 10 stars returned a WCS wrong by ≥ 15″ at the corners. Short of interpolation it stays too liberal with outliers among a few dozen matches: on 1,200 undistorted frames of 12–50 stars, n ≥ 2k chose distortion 16 times (corner errors up to 107″), n ≥ 3k 5 times, n ≥ 4k once (3″); a BIC margin of up to 50 instead of a count still left 3 crates/fayde-solve/src/fit.rs (fit_best_order)
    Similarity fits are repeated with the tangent point moved to the fitted CRVAL until it stops moving A transform found in another point's tangent plane is rotated by (east offset × tan δ) at CRVAL (convergence of the meridians). Measured: median hypotheses per hinted solve at Dec 86–89° fell from 607 to 6, the same as at low declination crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (similarity_seed)
    Distortion tune-up: a hypothesis that is not conclusive under a linear model, or that matches over 1.25× (+3) as many stars at 12 px as inside its own gates, is re-matched at 12 px with the order chosen by BIC until no more stars join, then at 6 and 3 px; the better-scoring of the linear and tuned models is kept, and the tune-up counts as one more hypothesis Fayde's own. With lens distortion a linear model leaves the outer stars outside the match gate. astrometry.net also refits a promising match before deciding (tune-up in solver.c, recalled from the source), without distortion. Measured: 5% distortion with 0.3 px centroids went from 18/30 solved (4 of them wrong by > 3″) to 30/30, none wrong crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (tune, try_hypothesis)
    A distortion polynomial is kept only if its confident matches cover the sources: in a 3 × 3 grid, every cell with ≥ 6 sources must have a matched fraction ≥ 0.25 × the frame's overall matched fraction; otherwise TAN, or no solution if the hypothesis only passed with distortion A polynomial locked onto part of the frame was wrong by up to 80 px in the rest (10% distortion test). Good fits had a lowest cell ratio of 0.47, partial locks ≤ 0.13 crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (covers_sources, final_fit)
    The fitted WCS must itself clear the acceptance threshold; the final IRLS runs until the match set and error scale repeat (at most 20 iterations, at least 4) The returned solution should be the one that passed, at a converged fit crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (final_fit)
    SIP inverse AP/BP of order forward + 2, fitted on a 25×25 grid; Fayde itself inverts by fixed-point iteration on A/B (exact to 1e-10 px) A quartic leaves 0.03 px at the corners of a cubic barrel; order + 2 gives < 0.01 px for external FITS readers crates/fayde-wcs/src/sip.rs
    Settle the match set with two TAN iterations before letting BIC consider SIP Avoids fitting distortion to a match set chosen by a wrong model crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (final_fit)
    A distortion order is kept only if no lower order predicts held-out cells of the frame (3 × 3, each left out in turn, training sets at n ≥ 2k) within 5% of its squared error; the lowest such order is used. Matches in fewer than 4 cells allow no distortion; when the order itself cannot be scored, BIC's order stands Spatial block cross-validation: Roberts et al. 2017, Ecography 40, 913. On simulated undistorted frames whose matches carry errors in one region, BIC took order 3 and the polynomial ran up to 146 px off in the corners crates/fayde-solve/src/fit.rs (held_out_order), crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (supported_order)
    When the request has a scale or roll prior, a final fit that ends with distortion is converged again as TAN with scale and roll free of the priors, matching afresh. Both are scored on the same sources at the distortion fit's error scale. A TAN outside the hint's hard limits replaces the distortion fit if it explains the sources at least as well (the hard limits then refuse it); a TAN inside them replaces it only with higher posterior odds, priors included Fayde's own. With the hint's scale wrong by more than its limits, the scale stopped inside them and distortion made up the rest with partly wrong matches (42.7 px off in the corners, log-odds 394); a trailed frame with smear did the same (30.7 px). With both checks the first gives no solution and the second solves to 0.03 px; of 106 simulated frames solved with distortion, 8 more are correct and none worse; 178 of 186 real-frame solutions stay bit-identical and the other 8 move closer to the Gaia references crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (converge)
    Header priors order: XPIXSZ/FOCALLEN, then a unit-checked linear WCS (CD, or PC × CDELT), then PIXSCALE/SECPIX; headers with both CD and PC give no scale; pointing from CRVAL then RA/DEC then OBJCTRA/OBJCTDEC; time from JD-OBS, MJD-OBS, then DATE-OBS (+TIME-OBS), all + EXPTIME/2 for mid-exposure No published source; CUNIT = 'degree(s)' accepted alongside FITS deg because some real headers use it crates/fayde-fits/src/priors.rs
    Tangent-plane inverse defined for every input: beyond 1e150 rad the reference-point term is dropped and only the direction of the offset is kept (the limit of the gnomonic inverse, 90° from the reference point); a NaN offset gives the reference point A WCS or pixel at the edge of the f64 range gave a panic; the limit is the mathematically defined answer crates/fayde-wcs/src/tan.rs (Basis::deproject)
    Header numbers must be finite: NaN, inf and exponents past the f64 range are kept as text, so a numeric read of that keyword is an error naming it A non-finite WCS or SIP value must not reach the arithmetic, and must not be read as an absent term crates/fayde-wcs/src/header.rs (parse_value), crates/fayde-wcs/src/sip.rs
    Repeated keyword: the first card is the one looked up Matches the lookup of cfitsio and Astropy, so a header that repeats a keyword gives the same WCS in each crates/fayde-wcs/src/header.rs (Header::get)
    Plausible epoch range, Julian years 1800 to 2200: a header clock outside it is reported and skipped, a request or index epoch outside it is an error A Julian Date in MJD-OBS or an unset camera clock otherwise moves every star by centuries of proper motion and fails with an unrelated message crates/fayde-core/src/epoch.rs, crates/fayde-fits/src/priors.rs, crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (validate)
    Header pointing is used only when the header does not declare another frame: RADESYS other than ICRS or FK5, or EQUINOX other than 2000, drops it with a warning (a bare EPOCH is not consulted: it is also written for the observation date); right ascension outside [0°, 360°) or 24 h is rejected, not wrapped No precession is applied, so B1950 or equinox-of-date coordinates are up to about 0.7° from ICRS; a prior must not be those coordinates read as ICRS crates/fayde-fits/src/priors.rs (foreign_frame, header_priors)
    Blind hypotheses whose implied frame exceeds twice the index field size are skipped before any catalogue query An index cannot hold patterns larger than its fields; such hypotheses produced sky-sized cones (found in a comparison run on real frames) crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (pattern_hash)
    Catalogue cones cached per (HEALPix cell of the field centre, √2 field-radius bucket) Avoids one catalogue query per blind hypothesis crates/fayde-solve/src/solve.rs (pattern_hash)
    Catalogue stars of a pointing prior read as hypotheses need them: only the brightest stars of the prior's cone for seeding (Catalog::cone_brightest), and every star in a cone of 1.25 × the frame's radius around a hypothesis that passes the pre-filter; after 6 such reads the rest of the prior's cone is read in one go A 2° prior around a 0.6° frame covers 46 frame areas, and reading, propagating and sorting all of it was three quarters of a hinted solve.     Considered and not adopted¶

    Algorithm Source Why not
    A pattern-hash stage ahead of the pair seeds when a pointing, a tight scale and an index are all given Fayde's own Measured on 146 real source lists of 4 to 60 sources with the two all-sky indexes. The hash stage solved 123 of the 134 solvable lists and halved the median (1.3 ms against 2.8 ms), but a stage that finds nothing costs 37 to 395 ms in a 17 GB table (1 ms in a regional one, which is what first suggested the idea), and a match that comes late costs up to 86 ms: mean 24.0 ms against 4.5 ms with the pair seeds first. A cap on the stage gains under 10%. Pair seeds solved every list the hash did
    Radon-transform streak search (fast Radon transform; median Radon) Nir, Zackay & Ofek 2018, AJ 156, 229; Stark et al. 2022, ACS ISR 2022-08 Finds long streaks below the per-pixel noise, at about 0.9 to 10 s per megapixel after stars are removed. Fayde needs streaks kept out of the star list, which only streaks above the threshold threaten.
    Hough transform on thresholded pixels (kernel-based: Fernandes & Oliveira 2008; Rubin maskStreaks) Pattern Recognition 41, 299; DMTN-197 Each ridge-like peak already carries its direction, so a trace from it replaces the vote.
    A-contrario alignment of points Lezama et al. 2015, IEEE TPAMI 37(3), 499 For grouping fragments of a faint streak; the tracer follows the pixels instead. Its significance test would suit a fragment-only detector.
    Exact null distribution of peak heights with Benjamini–Hochberg selection (STEM) Schwartzman, Gavrilov & Adler 2011, Ann. Stat. 39, 3290; Cheng & Schwartzman 2017 The continuous-field formula is 25 to 35% conservative for a kernel of about 1 px (Lin, Schwartzman & Davenport 2025, arXiv:2501.13239); the pixel bound is tighter there and is used with it.
    Iterative Gaussian-windowed centroid Bertin & Arnouts 1996 (SExtractor XWIN) Equal accuracy on the matched-filtered peak at a fraction of the cost.
    4-D quad codes searched in a kd-tree Lang et al. 2010 Fayde's 5 sorted edge ratios describe the same four-point shape as a quad code, and the hash lookup already enumerates neighbouring bins and re-checks the exact shape, which is the continuous-tolerance behaviour a kd-tree gives. What astrometry.net adds for blind solving is its scale-band index series, which Fayde adopts.
